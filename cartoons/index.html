Water Safety Cartoons for Kids

Two-minute cartoons with Buoy the capybara and friends. Watch together, then say the rule out loud.

Episodes

Each cartoon tells one short story and ends with one rule your child can remember. They are free to watch, and each runs under three minutes.

Episode 3 · 2 min 39 sec

Weekend with Nana Flo

Gus leaves the kids with Nana Flo for the weekend. She's sure they can all swim, but nobody told her who swims well, who needs a noodle, or who is watching.

Today's rule: Who is watching? Say it out loud!

For grown-ups: The grandparent water safety checklist

Episode 2 · 2 min 41 sec

Nana Flo's Thirty Minutes

Nana Flo says everyone must wait thirty minutes after lunch before they swim. So everyone watches the kitchen timer, and nobody watches little Ziggy.

Today's rule: A grown-up watches, every time!

For grown-ups: Swimming after eating, myth vs. reality

How to Watch Together

  1. Sit down and watch with your child. A cartoon lands better when a grown-up is watching too.
  2. Pause when Buoy asks, "What could we do differently?" Let your child answer first.
  3. Say the rule out loud together at the end. Then use the same words at the pool.

Meet the Friends

The same six friends show up in every story.

Buoy

The host. A capybara who loves the water.

Gus

A hippo dad and the Water Watcher.

Nana Flo

A flamingo grandma with a big heart.

Tilly

A careful rabbit who asks good questions.

Scout

A penguin who knows a lot and likes to say so.

Ziggy

A curious little raccoon, always on the move.

For Grown-Ups

Every rule in a cartoon comes from one of our free water safety guides, so you can read the facts behind it. A cartoon is a way to start the talk. It does not replace a grown-up watching the water, or swim lessons.

The cartoons are made with AI animation tools. A person reviews every episode before it goes out.

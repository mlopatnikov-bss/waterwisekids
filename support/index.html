Support WaterWiseKids

Our mission, nonprofit plan, and information for prospective supporters

Water safety should be accessible to every family

A public resource with a plan to do more

WaterWiseKids publishes free, practical water safety guidance and helps families find swim lessons. We are exploring a nonprofit structure to expand this work with accountable, mission directed funding.

Read the prospective supporter briefReview the public baseline

Where we stand today

WaterWiseKids is currently owned and operated by VTL Holding LLC. A separate nonprofit organization has not yet been formed or recognized as tax exempt. We are sharing this plan so prospective supporters can evaluate the work and discuss future opportunities.

WWK is not currently accepting charitable gifts through this site, and payments to WWK should not be treated as tax deductible donations. We will update this page with the new entity’s legal name, registration details, governance, and giving options if and when those steps are complete.

Who we are and why this matters

WWK is a national water safety education website built by swim instructors and water safety educators. Families can use our free guides, tools and printables, and swim lesson directory without a fee.

The CDC identifies drowning as the leading cause of death for children ages 1–4. Clear, accessible information can help families understand layers of protection and find appropriate instruction. We do not claim that website visits alone prevent drownings; that outcome requires careful evaluation.

What future support could fund

Evidence and editorial review

Maintain and update free guides as safety guidance changes, with clearer citations and review dates.

Access and usability

Improve mobile reading, accessible tools, and translated resources where community needs and qualified review support them.

Directory quality

Keep lesson and pool information useful by improving correction workflows and clearly identifying what WWK has verified.

Measurement

Establish repeatable reporting on content, reach, use, and feedback, then assess whether resources help families take safer actions.

These are proposed priorities, not funded programs or promises of a particular outcome. A future nonprofit would publish project scopes and budgets before seeking restricted support.

What a supporter can expect

Prospective supporters can review a concise partner brief, the public baseline dataset (CSV), and the resource catalog. A future partner could help underwrite a defined community education project, receive a written scope and milestones, and use agreed project materials with the communities or employees it serves. We propose reporting on activities and spending, with a final summary of results and limits. Recognition, if offered, would be clearly labeled and would never give a funder control over safety advice or standard directory ranking.

Organizations seeking promotion can review our separate advertising options. A paid school spotlight is an advertising purchase, even if its proceeds support the mission; it is not presented as a charitable donation.

Public baseline and measurement plan

We have not published audited finances, donor counts, independent outcome studies, or verified audience analytics. We will identify the source and limits of any future claim.

Independence and corporate affiliation

VTL Holding LLC owns WaterWiseKids and the British Swim School of Jersey Shore and Northwest Philadelphia operations. Those schools may benefit from enrollment through WWK. We disclose the affiliation on relevant school pages and label paid promotion. Our funding and editorial policy explains how we separate safety content, standard listings, and advertising.

Any future nonprofit arrangement involving WWK, VTL Holding LLC, or affiliated schools will need documented governance and conflict review. We will publish the resulting structure before describing WWK as a nonprofit.

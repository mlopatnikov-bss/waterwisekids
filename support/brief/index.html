Prospective Supporter Brief

A concise review of the mission, opportunity, evidence, and current status

← Return to Support WWK ·

Prospective supporter brief · November 7, 2026

Help more families access useful water safety information

WaterWiseKids is building a public resource for families and considering a nonprofit structure to expand access, quality, and accountability.

At a glance

Current operator: VTL Holding LLC. Proposed direction: a separate nonprofit entity for mission directed water safety work. Status: formation, tax exemption, governance, and charitable solicitation readiness have not been completed. WWK is not currently accepting charitable gifts through this site.

Current public resources: free educational articles, practical tools, and a swim lesson directory. Our baseline dataset and resource catalog inventory 641 education article files and directory pages for 50 states and DC; these are output counts, not measures of reach or safety outcomes.

The need

Drowning is the leading cause of death for children ages 1–4, according to the CDC. Families need clear guidance on supervision, barriers, water competency, and emergency readiness. WWK’s role is to make practical information easier to find and understand, while pointing families to swim instruction they can assess for themselves.

Proposed investment areas

Keep information current

Scheduled expert review, citations, and clear update dates for high priority guides.

Improve access

Usable mobile tools, accessible formats, and translations with qualified review.

Improve discovery

Directory corrections, clearer verification labels, and easier comparison of programs.

Measure use

Define reach and engagement metrics, collect feedback, and test whether resources support safer decisions.

Specific projects would require a scope, budget, timeline, and appropriate nonprofit or fiscal arrangements before a funding request.

How a partner could create value

A future partner could support a defined community education project that fits its geographic priorities, share agreed project materials with the families or employees it serves, and receive clearly labeled acknowledgement where appropriate. We would set the scope and usage rights in writing.

How we would report value

For each future project, we propose to agree on a small set of measurable deliverables and report progress against them. Possible measures include resources reviewed or translated, accessibility improvements, directory corrections, qualified audience reach, and user feedback. We would identify data sources and limitations and distinguish outputs from observed behavior and safety outcomes.

WWK has not published audited financial statements, independently verified reach, or an outcome study. These gaps are part of the work ahead, not results to imply today.

Independence and recognition

VTL Holding LLC also owns British Swim School of Jersey Shore and Northwest Philadelphia operations. This relationship appears on relevant WWK pages. Future governance would need to address related party transactions and editorial independence. Any supporter recognition would be labeled; it would not influence safety recommendations or standard directory ranking.

Paid promotional placements are advertising purchases. They are separate from a charitable contribution and do not become a donation because WWK uses revenue for mission related work. See the funding and editorial policy.

Start a conversation

Share your organization’s goals and the communities you hope to support. We can discuss a future project and the documentation needed before any charitable giving.

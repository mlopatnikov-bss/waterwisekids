Swim Level Crosswalk

Compare six lesson systems by broad skill band—not by marketing name

Use this as a conversation starter, not a placement decision. Level names are not standardized. Age tracks, teaching methods, pools, and graduation tests differ. A receiving school must assess the swimmer before enrollment.

This public beta maps provider-published descriptions to seven neutral skill bands. A match means the descriptions overlap; it does not mean the levels are identical or that a swimmer has demonstrated every skill.

Translate a level

Methodology and guardrails

  • Only current official provider or curriculum-owner pages are used.
  • Each description is mapped to one band or a range. Broad provider categories receive lower confidence.
  • Age-specific tracks remain separate. A similar skill band does not make an infant, preschool, and school-age class interchangeable.
  • No provider pays for its mapping, order, or confidence label. Corrections require an official source.
  • The dataset expires for review after 90 days. It is withdrawn or relabeled stale if the source cannot be rechecked.

Official sources

  • Aqua-Tots: Aqua-Tots Swim Journey — checked 2026-11-11. Current national level page; local placement and offerings may vary.
  • British Swim School: British Swim School lesson assessment — checked 2026-11-11. Current local assessment page lists the national progression; local placement and offerings may vary.
  • Goldfish Swim School: Goldfish guide to skill levels — checked 2026-11-11. The current guide describes broad level families rather than every numbered local level; matches are intentionally ranges.
  • SafeSplash: SafeSplash lesson levels explained — checked 2026-11-11. Current national 4 C's framework; exact class placement depends on age, skills, goals, and location.
  • American Red Cross: Red Cross swim lessons for children — checked 2026-11-11. Age tracks are separate progressions; a band match does not make Preschool and Learn-to-Swim levels interchangeable.
  • YMCA: YMCA water safety and swim lessons — checked 2026-11-11. The national page defines three broad categories; local Y stage names and placement rules vary.

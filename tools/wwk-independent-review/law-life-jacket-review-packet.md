### Florida — `fl-child-life-jacket-20261111`

> On Florida waters, a person may not operate a vessel less than 26 feet long while it is underway unless every child under 6 on board is wearing a U.S. Coast Guard-approved personal flotation device used according to its approval label. For this rule, underway excludes a vessel that is anchored, moored, made fast to shore, or aground.

- Child Age: under 6
- Vessel Scope: vessels less than 26 feet
- Water Scope: waters of Florida
- Operating Condition: while underway
- Explicit Exception Or Definition: underway excludes anchored, moored, made fast to shore, or aground

### New Jersey — `nj-child-life-jacket-20261111`

> On New Jersey waters, a vessel operator must ensure every child age 12 or younger on board wears a properly fitted U.S. Coast Guard-approved personal flotation device whenever the vessel is underway. The New Jersey State Police equipment page states that the rule does not apply when every child under 13 is inside an enclosed cabin.

- Child Age: 12 or younger
- Vessel Scope: vessels
- Water Scope: waters of New Jersey
- Operating Condition: while underway
- Explicit Exception Or Definition: enclosed-cabin exception stated by New Jersey State Police

### Pennsylvania — `pa-child-life-jacket-20261111`

> On Pennsylvania waters, children age 12 or younger must wear a U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jacket while underway in any boat 20 feet or less long and in all canoes, kayaks, and paddleboards. Separate Pennsylvania rules also require broader life-jacket wear for specified craft during the November 1 through April 30 cold-water period.

- Child Age: 12 or younger
- Vessel Scope: boats 20 feet or less and all canoes, kayaks, and paddleboards
- Water Scope: Pennsylvania waters
- Operating Condition: while underway
- Explicit Exception Or Definition: separate seasonal and location-specific wear rules also apply

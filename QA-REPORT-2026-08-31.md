## Probe note — the first FAQ parity result was wrong

The initial schema↔visible sweep reported **23 mismatches** across `scholarships/`, `special-needs-swimming.html` and two `swimmers-hub/` guides. All 23 were false positives from my own normalizer, not site defects:

- Visible headings carry a `Q: ` lead-in (`<h3 class="faq-question">Q: My child has severe anxiety…`) that the normalizer did not strip.
- `scholarships/index.html` uses a **third** FAQ markup shape — `<button class="faq-question">` with a nested `<span>` arrow — beyond the two known shapes (`h3`, `p > strong`).

Re-run with `^[QA][:.]` stripping, `button`/`.faq-question` included, and nested `<span>` dropped: **0 mismatches**, against a canary of 21,290 visible headings. Worth folding both into the standard probe — reporting those 23 as defects would have sent a fix run at healthy pages.

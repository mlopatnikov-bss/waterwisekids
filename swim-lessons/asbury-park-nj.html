Frequently Asked Questions

What age should kids start swim lessons in Asbury Park?

The American Academy of Pediatrics says most children are ready for formal swim lessons around age 4, and children as young as age 1 can begin water-familiarization classes. In a shore community, starting early helps children build comfort and safety skills before regular beach and pool exposure.

Do swim lessons reduce drowning risk for young children?

Yes. The American Academy of Pediatrics reports that formal swim lessons can reduce the risk of drowning by 88% for children ages 1 to 4. The CDC notes that drowning is the leading cause of unintentional death for children ages 1 to 4, which makes early lessons especially important.

How often should my child take swim lessons?

Weekly lessons are the most common cadence and are enough for steady progress for most beginners. Year-round consistency matters more than frequency, so continuing lessons through the fall and winter helps children retain skills between beach seasons.

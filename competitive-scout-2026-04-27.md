## 1. Company Snapshot

- **Founder:** Lenny Krayzelburg — born in Odesa, Ukraine; immigrated to the U.S. with his family escaping Soviet anti-Semitism; USC finance graduate. Credentials:
  - Four-time Olympic gold medalist (2000, 2004 Games)
  - 1999 and 2000 USA Swimming Swimmer of the Year
  - 1998 World Champion in 100m and 200m back
  - 5 world records in backstroke
  - 2004 Olympic Swim Team Captain
  - 2011 International Swimming Hall of Fame inductee
  - USC Sports Hall of Fame member
  - Treasurer, Michael Phelps Foundation board
- **Locations:** Two corporate-owned LA pools — **West Hills** (de Toledo High School, 22622 Vanowen St) and **West Los Angeles** (Westside JCC, 5870 W. Olympic Blvd). Franchise/partner footprint claims: Miami, Brooklyn, Boston, Dallas; early JCCA-licensed satellites in Texas, Illinois, New Jersey, New York, Massachusetts.
- **Ownership model:** Founder-operated by an Olympian, originally JCCA-licensed satellites, now a franchise system (post-2018 rebrand). Distinct from PE-backed Aqua-Tots/Goldfish or educator-owned regional schools like La Petite Baleen.
- **Age range:** As young as 3 months old (with healed umbilical cord and pediatrician approval).
- **Track record claim:** "We have successfully taught over 40,000 children with essential water safety skills."

## 2. The SwimRight® Method — Levels & Distinctions

**Core definition (verbatim):** *"A learn-to-swim curriculum that uses the swim, float, swim technique as its foundation. Before teaching any of the actual strokes, the SwimRight Method focuses on providing children with the knowledge required to be confident and safe in and around the water. All of our students are first taught the skill of rolling over onto their backs to breathe."*

**Distinguishing claim (verbatim):** *"While other swim methods teach children to breathe by lifting their heads up, we have found that only makes them tired. If a child can relax and rest in their back float then they have a much better chance of survival."*

**Two programs, 9 published levels** (FAQ also says "8 levels of learning" — discrepancy likely because Flyers is the gold capstone):

**SwimSafe Program (water-safety foundation):**
1. **Splashers** — pool-environment introduction, comfort building.
2. **Floaters** — establish unlimited back-float comfort.
3. **Kickers** — independent movement with proper body position and kicking.
4. **Kickboarders** — rotational drills with arm use.
5. **Rollers** — further rotational drills, body position, kicking.
6. **Streamliners** — catch-up freestyle/backstroke, intro breaststroke/butterfly kick.

**SwimRight Program (stroke development):**
7. **Freestylers (bronze)** — fully developed freestyle and backstroke.
8. **Gliders (silver)** — proper technique full FR/BK; develop BR/BF; starts and turns.
9. **Flyers (gold)** — full breaststroke and butterfly; advanced drills, starts, turns.

Tagline: *"All doors to swimming and beyond, from SwimSafe to SwimRight to SwimFast to SwimFastest!"*

**SwimRight vs. ISR comparison:** Both teach back-floating as the survival anchor. ISR is intensive (10–15 min daily, 4–6 weeks, instructor-only). SwimRight is gradual, year-round, perpetual-billing, group-by-default — rolling-to-back is the foundational skill from day one and remains central across all 9 levels.

## 3. Age Policies

- **Youngest:** 3 months old, contingent on healed umbilical cord and pediatrician approval.
- **Why-young rationale (verbatim):** *"Babies have not yet developed specific fears, and the pool is a womb-like environment… Young babies have an automatic breath holding reflex and are more comfortable on their backs… This dynamic can change as early as 3-6 months or when they start rolling over."*
- **Parent-Tot:** Optional class for children under 2.
- **Transition:** Parent-Tot → private (under-2s) → group lessons once child has no anxiety.

## 4. Class Formats & Ratios

- **Group lessons:** Strongly recommended default. Verbatim: *"We typically only recommended private lesson for infants and toddlers or those that are working with fear."*
- **Private lessons:** **20 minutes** (shorter than the 30-min industry norm). Justification: short attention span and infant fatigue.
- **Parent-Tot:** Under-2 entry path.
- **Ratios:** Not published. Implied small-group with in-water (not deck-side) instructors.

## 8. Safety & Survival Skills

**Survival sequence (verbatim from FAQ):** *"Within our teaching curriculum we have 2 survival tests… Both tests are performed while kids are fully clothed. They enter the pool by surprise and use the skills that they have learned to save themselves. The first survival test requires the child to roll to their back and float for an unlimited amount of time. The second survival test requires the child to roll on their back, rest, and then swim back to the wall."*

So the taught sequence is: **Roll → Float (unlimited) → Swim-to-Wall** — i.e. swim-float-swim with rotational rollover as the anchor skill. Notable: surprise pool entry while fully clothed (real-world simulation) — only Goldfish and ISR have similarly intense survival demonstrations on record.

**No flotation devices (verbatim):** *"Floatation devices can create a false sense of security… It is far better for children to learn the proper skills needed to save themselves in an emergency."*

**Drowning stat cited:** Standard CDC drowning-leading-cause statistic for ages 1–4 and second-leading-cause-under-14.

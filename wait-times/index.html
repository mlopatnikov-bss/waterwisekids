WaterWiseKids · Open data pilot

Swim lesson wait-time tracker

Help document how long families are being asked to wait for a suitable class. A report starts verification; it does not become a published fact automatically.

No wait-time statistic is publishable yet. The collection currently has zero verified observations. WWK will not publish a market average, trend or named-school ranking from anecdotes.

Submit a current observation

Use a current provider page, a direct provider answer, or your own recent experience. Do not submit student names, schedules, health information, screenshots of private messages, payment details or account credentials.

Submission does not guarantee publication. WWK may contact the provider through its public business channel to corroborate the report. Aggregate data will carry sampling limitations.

Publication gates

GateRequirement
Market coverageAt least 30 current observations and three provider types
FreshnessEvery included observation checked within 45 days
ConcentrationNo provider above 30% of the market sample
ReviewA separate reviewer checks source, date, class context and normalization
PrivacyAggregate cells under five observations are withheld; no student-level data is published

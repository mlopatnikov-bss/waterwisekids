Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find swim lessons near me?

Start broad by choosing your region, then filter by your child's age and lesson type before opening the local page that fits your family. WaterWiseKids organizes lessons by city, county, shore town, and age so you can move from a general search to specific, beginner-friendly options.

What age should my child start swim lessons?

The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends most children begin formal swim lessons after their first birthday, when increasing mobility raises the risk of unintended water access. Readiness varies by child, so age is a guide rather than a strict cutoff.

Why do swim lessons matter for young children?

Drowning is the leading cause of unintentional death for children ages 1-4, according to the CDC. The AAP cites research showing that formal swim lessons can reduce drowning risk by 88% for young children, making early, consistent lessons an important layer of water safety.

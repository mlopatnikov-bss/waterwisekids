# WWK mention-share engine

This is the operating brief for YouTube, Reddit, Facebook and community work.
It does not authorize automated posting. A person reviews the current sources,
conflicts and final copy before any account publishes or replies.

## Non-negotiable disclosure

Use this first-person disclosure when relevant:

> I publish WaterWiseKids and also operate British Swim School businesses
> serving the Jersey Shore and Northwest Philadelphia. That affiliation may
> financially benefit me, so I separate it from the general answer and label
> any relevant link.

Do not claim that the publisher is a physician, independent reviewer or holder
of a professional credential unless the public author profile has verified it.

## YouTube release format

Each episode answers one parent question, names one primary source or WWK
dataset, states its limitation on screen, and links to the methodology. Add
chapters and a corrected transcript. Do not state a national Price Index number
while the publication gates fail.

Initial six-episode queue:

1. **When can swim lessons start?** AAP after-first-birthday readiness framing;
   infant lessons are not proven to prevent drowning.
2. **Why WWK will not publish a national swim-lesson average yet.** Show the
   17-observation pilot and its unmet coverage gates.
3. **Switching swim schools without guessing a level.** Demonstrate the
   Crosswalk, then require receiving-school assessment.
4. **Pool-fence “law by state” pages often overclaim.** Show the three verified
   Law Atlas jurisdictions and why unverified states are withheld.
5. **What to compare in the complete price.** Tuition, lesson count, duration,
   required fees, membership, commitment and class format.
6. **How to ask a school about transparency.** Credential issuer, maximum ratio
   by level, background-check policy, price and make-up policy; disclosure is
   not safety certification.

## Reddit and community rules

- Use an established, disclosed identity. Never create supporting personas,
  coordinate votes or manufacture testimonials.
- Answer the question before mentioning WWK. Link only when the specific page
  adds sources, a dataset or a tool the answer cannot reasonably reproduce.
- Do not recommend an affiliated school without the ownership disclosure.
- Do not give medical, legal or tax conclusions for an individual family.
- Preserve the community URL, date, final text and any correction in the
  outreach log. Respect each community's self-promotion rules.

## Facebook price-check collection

A price-check thread may request provider, location, product type, lesson
count, duration, tuition, required fee and source date. It must say that parent
reports are leads for verification—not verified Price Index observations. Do
not request a child's name, schedule, medical information, receipt image with
personal details, payment data or account credentials in a public thread.

## Measurement

Use campaign tags by episode or community response. Track linked citations,
unlinked mentions and referral visits separately. A platform mention is not
evidence that the statement is correct, and a referral is not proof of an AI
citation. Reconcile outcomes with the frozen 100-prompt panel under
`tools/wwk-ai-visibility/`.

# WWK independent review recruitment brief

## Why this role exists

WaterWiseKids publishes parent-facing water-safety and swim-lesson material.
The site needs an independent reviewer for high-risk medical and safety claims;
the site owner and AI tools must not be the final authority on those claims.

## Minimum qualifications

Recruit one of the following, with verifiable current credentials:

- a pediatrician or pediatric injury-prevention clinician with drowning-
  prevention experience; or
- a credentialed aquatics professional with documented water-safety program
  leadership, with medical questions still referred to a clinician.

The reviewer must be independent of the owner's British Swim School businesses
and disclose any other relevant commercial relationships.

## Initial scope

- Review the canonical facts file and its AAP/AHA/Red Cross citations.
- Review the pediatrician handout, age/readiness guidance, CPR/emergency pages,
  survival-swim comparisons, and drowning terminology.
- Mark each item `approved`, `changes requested`, or `outside scope`, with a
  review date and source notes.
- Re-review after material source updates and at least annually.

The exact first review set and its current SHA-256 values are generated at
`tools/wwk-independent-review/medical-safety-review-packet.md`. A reviewer must
use that packet so approval cannot silently carry forward after a page changes.

## Publication rule

WWK may name a reviewer or display a “reviewed by” line only after the person
has accepted the role, supplied credentials, disclosed conflicts, and approved
the exact published revision. Until then, the public Medical Review Policy must
continue to say that universal independent review is not yet claimed.

## Outreach template

Subject: Independent water-safety reviewer for WaterWiseKids

WaterWiseKids is building a free, source-linked resource for families choosing
swim instruction and understanding drowning-prevention guidance. We are
seeking a paid independent reviewer for a defined set of medical and safety
pages. The role is review and correction—not endorsement of any swim school.
Would you be open to discussing scope, credentials, conflicts, turnaround time,
and compensation?

The dated candidate shortlist at
`docs/reviewer-recruitment/candidate-shortlist-2026-11-11.md` prioritizes current
AAP policy authors and a regional injury-prevention specialist using official
institutional profiles. It is research only; no candidate is represented as
available or affiliated with WWK.

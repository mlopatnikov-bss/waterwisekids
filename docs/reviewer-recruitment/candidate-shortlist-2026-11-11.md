## Contact sequence

1. Ask candidate 1; allow five business days for a response.
2. If unavailable, ask candidate 2, then candidate 3.
3. Contact candidate 4 in parallel for the nonclinical safety/usability portion,
   or ask CHOP's Injury Prevention Program to nominate an appropriate reviewer.

Do not mass-email all candidates, imply an existing relationship, or present a
name publicly before acceptance and exact-revision approval.

## Exact initial request

Attach or link the generated
`tools/wwk-independent-review/medical-safety-review-packet.md`. Ask for:

- availability and the candidate's rate for a fixed nine-item review;
- conflicts, including swim-school, safety-product and advocacy relationships;
- decisions of `approved`, `changes_requested` or `outside_scope` for every item;
- source/correction notes for every requested change;
- permission terms for displaying the reviewer's name, credentials and review
  date only on revisions they approve;
- expected turnaround and annual/material-change re-review availability.

The role is paid review and correction, not a testimonial, certification or
endorsement of WWK or any affiliated swim school.

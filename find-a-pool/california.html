Family Swimming Pools in California

5 pools in California checked on each operator's official website. Filter by indoor, year-round, lessons and who can swim.

Every pool below was checked on its operator's own website (the city, town, county, park district, YMCA or JCC) on October 12, 2026. We only show a detail, such as indoor, year-round, open swim, a day pass or an age rule, when the operator states it. Pools open to the public are listed first; pools whose public access isn't confirmed, and members-, residents- or guests-only pools, are listed separately and clearly marked. "Not stated by the operator" means we couldn't confirm it, not that the answer is no. Outdoor pools are usually closed outside their summer season, and none of these listings means a pool is open today. Hours and fees change, so each listing links to the official page for current details.

Coverage: 5 verified pools, 5 open to the public; 1 with an indoor pool, 3 with an outdoor pool. This is a starting list, not every pool in the state. The operators don't say whether 1 of these is indoor or outdoor. See all states.
Offers

Filters only show pools where the operator confirms that detail, for the same pool at the venue. Pools whose page doesn't say are hidden while a filter is on, not ruled out.

Showing pools open to the public first. Pools for members, residents or guests only, and pools whose public access isn't confirmed, are hidden; choose All pools under "Who can swim" to include them.

Pools open to the public (5)

Anyone can pay to swim at these pools. Some have limits, such as dates, hours, ticket rules or age rules, shown on each card. Where a venue has more than one pool, the card says which pool is open to the public.

Indoor or outdoor
Not stated by the operator

Pool: Seasonal (not open all year)
Competition pool: Not stated by the operator
Who can swim

Pool: open to the public
Competition pool: open to the public, with conditions

  • Anyone can pay daily admission
Before you go
  • Recreational (family) swim is closed for the season and returns in spring 2027.
  • Swimmers must pass a deep-water swim test and buy a wristband to use the 50-meter pool, which is at least 7 feet deep.

Children

Offers

    • Adult lap swim is for swimmers 16 and older.

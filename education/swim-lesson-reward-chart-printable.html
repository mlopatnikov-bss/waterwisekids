👩‍👧 For Grown-Ups: How to Use This Chart

  • Reward effort, not just results. "You put your face in — that’s brave!" builds a swimmer faster than "Did you win?"
  • Let your child place the sticker. Owning the win makes the next lesson something to look forward to.
  • Celebrate showing up on the tough days. Consistency — not intensity — is what turns a nervous beginner into a confident, water-safe swimmer.
  • Keep the reward small and non-food. Extra pool play, choosing the bedtime story, or a trip to the library keeps motivation healthy.
  • Ask the instructor "what’s next?" so the chart always has a fresh skill to aim for.

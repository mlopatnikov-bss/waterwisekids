Swim Lesson Thanksgiving Week Planner

Holiday weeks change schedules. Confirm class dates, plan make-ups, keep skills fresh and keep a designated water watcher at every gathering.

🔁 Step 3: Make-Up Plan

  • Closed class Ask if it is made up, credited or missed.
  • Credit length Ask how long make-up credits last and write the deadline below.
  • Booking Ask whether make-ups must be booked in advance.
  • Level Ask whether a make-up must be in the same level or location.
  • Travel Tell the school about planned absences before you go.
  • Write it down Record every missed class and make-up date in the tracker.
Tip: take a screenshot of the holiday calendar or save the email that confirms your dates.

💪 Step 4: Keep Skills Fresh

  • Short and playful Five to ten minutes of supervised water play is plenty.
  • Bath-time bubbles Practice blowing bubbles and calm breaths before water splashes the face.
  • Arm’s reach Keep a young child within arm’s reach of an adult in any water.
  • Pool trip Choose a pool with lifeguards that welcomes children, if you go.
  • Ask the instructor Ask which skills to practice and which to leave for class.
  • Be kind Praise effort and avoid comparing your child with others.

🛡️ Step 5: Gatherings and Travel Water Safety

  • Water watcher Name an adult to watch children near water and swap at set times.
  • No distractions The water watcher puts the phone away and does not drink alcohol or multitask.
  • Close and latch Keep gates and doors to pools and hot tubs closed and latched.
  • Empty containers Empty tubs, buckets and wading pools after use.
  • Ask the host Ask about pool fences, rules and where children may go.
  • Know the number Know how to reach 911 or your local emergency number.
Children are never alone in or near water. A lesson does not replace adult supervision, and lessons do not make a child drown-proof, at any age. If a child is in trouble in the water, get help and call 911 or your local emergency number.

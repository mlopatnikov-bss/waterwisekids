Water-Safety Law Atlas

Verified government sources, plain-English summaries, and explicit gaps

Plain-English research, not legal advice. Codes and boating rules can change, and local amendments may control. Confirm the rule for the property address or trip with the enforcing agency before relying on this atlas.
Verified pilot: 3 of 51 jurisdictions. Only Florida, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania are published in this pilot. The remaining states and District of Columbia stay unpublished until their controlling government sources receive a second review. This page replaces the earlier unsourced “all 50 states” summaries; unpublished is safer than confident but unverified.

Florida

Residential pool-barrier framework

Florida's Residential Swimming Pool Safety Act requires a residential pool barrier at least 4 feet high on the outside. It must prevent a young child from crawling under, squeezing through, or climbing over; access gates must open away from the pool, self-close, and self-latch. Above-ground pool structures or access controls may serve as barriers only when the statute's conditions are met.

Local check: The statute sets statewide requirements, but a local building department may apply additional construction-code details. Confirm the permit and inspection rules for the property address.

2026 Florida Statutes § 515.29. Checked 2026-11-11.

New Jersey

Residential pool-barrier framework

New Jersey regulates residential pool barriers through its Uniform Construction Code. The Department of Community Affairs lists the 2024 New Jersey editions of the International Residential Code and referenced International Swimming Pool and Spa Code as current from August 17, 2026.

Local check: The official state page identifies the adopted code framework but does not reproduce every barrier dimension. Obtain the applicable section and any local requirements from the municipal construction official before building or altering a barrier.

New Jersey DCA — Current Construction Codes. Checked 2026-11-11.

Pennsylvania

Residential pool-barrier framework

Pennsylvania's Uniform Construction Code regulates swimming-pool construction statewide. The Commonwealth says swimming-pool and hot-tub installation requires a permit, and more than 90% of municipalities administer and enforce the UCC locally.

Local check: Ask the municipality or its certified third-party agency which adopted code sections and local amendments apply to the address. In opt-out municipalities, use the Commonwealth's listed enforcement process.

Pennsylvania — Apply for a UCC Building Permit. Enforcement source. Checked 2026-11-11.

Federal child life-jacket baseline

When a vessel is underway, federal law generally requires children under 13 to wear an appropriate U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jacket unless below deck or inside an enclosed cabin. A different state child-wear rule applies on waters under that state's jurisdiction.

U.S. Coast Guard Boating Safety FAQ, checked 2026-11-11.

How to verify a rule for your address

  1. Ask the city, township, or county building department which adopted code and local amendments apply to the exact address.
  2. Request the pool-barrier section and permit checklist in writing before purchasing or altering a fence, gate, cover, or alarm.
  3. For boating, check the state boating authority for child-wear ages, water-specific rules, personal-watercraft rules, and any federal-land overlay.
  4. Save the dated source and the official's written response with permit and inspection records.

Methodology and publication gate

  • Every published module links to a current government source and carries a checked date.
  • “Verified framework” means the official source establishes the controlling code system but does not reproduce every paid model-code dimension.
  • State-specific boating claims remain unpublished when only the federal baseline has been checked.
  • A second reviewer must compare each summary with the cited text before a new jurisdiction is published.
  • All entries expire for recheck after 90 days and material changes are recorded in the Corrections Log.

Safety standard versus legal minimum

A code-compliant barrier is only one layer. The Pool Barrier Self-Check is based on CPSC safety guidance and is not a permit inspection. Use active adult supervision, barriers, alarms or covers where appropriate, swim skills, life jackets around open water, and CPR preparation together.

Switching Swim Schools Checklist

A swim lesson is a safety program, so the bar for staying should be high. Use this one-page checklist to decide whether it's time to move, evaluate a new program, and switch without losing your child's progress.

WaterWiseKids.com — Free water safety education for families

🚩 Signs It May Be Time to Switch

  • Your child has plateaued for months with no clear reason and no plan to fix it.
  • Class sizes have grown until no instructor is truly watching your swimmer.
  • Lessons stop for the season and hard-won skills fade over the break.
  • The program can't tell you what your child has mastered or what comes next.
  • Instructors rotate or lack certification, or the water is so cold your child dreads it.
  • Your questions get brushed off — a safety-first program welcomes them.

✅ What a Stronger Program Offers

  • Certified, background-checked instructors trained in both teaching and water rescue.
  • Small student-to-instructor ratios so eyes stay on every swimmer.
  • Warm, indoor water that lets lessons run year-round instead of stopping each fall.
  • A safety-first, level-based curriculum that teaches survival skills before strokes.
  • Honest progress tracking and a placement assessment for transferring swimmers.
  • A free trial so you can test the fit before you commit.

❓ Ask a New School & Switch Smoothly

  • How do you assess and place a child coming from another program?
  • What is the ratio, schedule, and pricing — and do lessons run year-round in warm water?
  • List your child's current skills before you leave so the new instructor can pick up where they are.
  • Book a trial before the old lessons end so there's no long gap for skills to fade.
  • Frame it as a fresh start and tell the new instructor about any fears or medical needs.
  • Keep the first goodbye short — expect a lesson or two of adjustment, then progress.

Ready to Find a Better Fit?

If your current lessons have stalled or stop every fall, the fastest way to protect your child's progress is a safety-first, warm-water program that runs year-round. Compare options near you and start with a trial — consistent lessons are one of the most powerful layers of protection against drowning.

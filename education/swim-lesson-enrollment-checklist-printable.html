Swim Lesson Enrollment Checklist

Choosing the right swim program is a safety decision, not just a scheduling one. Use this one-page checklist to evaluate a program, ask the right questions, and start lessons with confidence.

WaterWiseKids.com — Free water safety education for families

✅ What to Look For in a Safe Program

  • Certified, background-checked instructors trained in both teaching and water rescue — ask what certifications they hold.
  • Small student-to-instructor ratios so your child gets real attention and eyes stay on every swimmer.
  • Warm, indoor water that lets lessons run year-round — skills fade during long seasonal breaks.
  • A clear, level-based curriculum that starts with water safety and survival skills, not just strokes.
  • Progress tracking so you can see what your child has mastered and what comes next.
  • A free trial or make-up policy — a good program lets you test the fit before you commit.

❓ Questions to Ask Before You Enroll

  • What is the class size and instructor ratio for my child's age and level?
  • How do you place a new child, and how does a swimmer move up a level?
  • What water safety and survival skills are taught first?
  • What are the schedule, pricing, make-up, and cancellation policies?
  • What happens if my child is nervous or cries at the first lesson?
  • Can we watch or try a lesson before enrolling?

🏊 Getting Ready for the First Lesson

  • Pack the swim bag: suit, towel, goggles, swim diaper if needed, and a dry change of clothes.
  • Talk it up ahead of time. Keep your tone calm and positive so your child arrives excited, not anxious.
  • Arrive early so there's time to change, use the restroom, and meet the instructor unhurried.
  • Tell the instructor about any fears, medical needs, or prior experience before class starts.
  • Keep goodbyes short and confident — a quick, cheerful handoff helps nervous swimmers settle.

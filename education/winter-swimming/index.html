Find winter swim lessons

Search the swim lesson finder by ZIP code, or browse the New Jersey and Pennsylvania directories. On those two state pages, listings now show whether the school's own location page confirms an indoor pool or year-round lessons, and you can filter for either. Where a school's page doesn't say, the listing says "not confirmed" rather than guessing.

The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends water heated to 87 to 94°F for classes with children age 3 and younger, so ask any school what temperature it keeps its pool.

Find a pool for winter swimming

Find a Pool lists 238 pools in all 50 states and Washington, DC, each checked against the operator's own website. It is a starting list, with 3 to 5 pools in most states so far. 210 are open to the public (some with limits, such as dates or age rules, shown on each listing); the rest are for members, residents or their guests, or their public access isn't confirmed, and are listed separately. 98 have a confirmed indoor pool (81 of them with an indoor pool confirmed open to the public), and 46 of the indoor pools are also confirmed open year-round. Each listing links to the operator's current hours and fees.

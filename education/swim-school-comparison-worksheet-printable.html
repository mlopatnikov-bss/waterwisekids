Before You Drive Anywhere — 5 Phone-Screen Questions

  • "What is your student-to-instructor ratio for my child's age?" Looking for 4:1 or smaller for beginners under 6; 1:1 with a parent in the water for infants.
  • "At what level do children learn to roll onto their back and breathe?" Survival skills should come early, not as an advanced reward.
  • "What is your teaching pool's water temperature?" 88–92°F keeps young children relaxed and learning. Quality schools know the number instantly.
  • "Can I observe a class before enrolling — and every lesson after?" Any hesitation on observation is a red flag. Ask about free trial lessons while you're at it.
  • "What does a month really cost?" Tuition + registration + annual fees + required gear. Ask about sibling discounts and makeup policies.

Red Flags — Two or More Ends the Tour

  • Parents not allowed to observe lessons — no legitimate reason exists for this.
  • Ratios above 6:1 for non-swimmers — one instructor cannot keep hands-on attention on six beginners.
  • No posted emergency procedures — or staff who cannot describe them when asked.
  • No written level progression — "every child is different" is not a curriculum.
  • Instructors unsure of their own certification — training should be a source of pride, not a mystery.
  • Shivering children in the current class — cold water means lessons children learn to dread.
  • Pressure to prepay a long contract before observing a class — confident schools let their teaching sell itself.

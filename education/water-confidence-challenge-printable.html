The 30-Day Water Confidence Challenge

One small, playful step a day — from face-in-the-water at home to floating, gliding, and swimming. Confidence is built in tiny, repeated wins, not big scary leaps.

WaterWiseKids.com — Free water safety education for families

✅ How to Use This Challenge

  • An adult stays within arm's reach for every water day — touch supervision, no exceptions, no phone.
  • Keep it short and fun — 5 to 10 minutes is plenty. Stop while your child still wants more.
  • Never force it. If a step feels too big, repeat yesterday's win instead. Repeating a day is progress, not failure.
  • Celebrate every check mark — a high-five, a sticker, a "you were so brave." Confidence grows on praise.

Week 1 — Get Comfortable (at home & bath time)

1

Pour a cup of water gently over the shoulders in the bath.

2

Blow bubbles in a bowl or sink like a "bubbling motor."

3

Practice a big breath in, hold it, and count to 3 — on dry land.

4

🌟 Reward day! Talk about what felt brave this week.

5

Pour water over the top of the head, count to 3, then wipe.

6

Wet the face with a cloth and wait 2 seconds before wiping eyes.

7

Blow bubbles with the mouth just touching the bath water.

Week 2 — Get In (shallow water)

8

Walk in to the waist holding an adult's hands. Splash and smile.

9

Play at the wall for 5 minutes — just get used to the water.

10

Blow bubbles at the surface with the chin in the water.

11

🌟 Reward day! Celebrate getting all the way in.

12

Dip chin, then mouth, then nose to the water, one at a time.

13

Hold the wall and do 10 gentle "splashy" kicks.

14

"Elevator" bobs: dip to the chin and pop back up, 5 times.

Week 3 — Float & Breathe

15

Put the whole face in and blow bubbles for 3 seconds.

16

Front float with an adult's hands under the tummy.

17

Back float with an adult gently supporting the head.

18

🌟 Reward day! How long can you float? Count together.

19

Rhythmic breathing: "bubble, bubble, breathe" × 5.

20

Big "star" float on the back, count to 5 with support.

21

Float, then reach and grab the wall to finish.

Week 4 — Move & Grow

22

Kick across a short distance with a kickboard or noodle.

23

Glide from the wall, arms stretched, for 2 seconds.

24

Roll from a front float to a back float with a little help.

25

🌟 Reward day! Jump to a waiting adult in shallow water.

26

Take 3–5 arm strokes to reach the wall.

27

Practice "swim, float, swim" — rest on a back float, then go.

28

Show-off day: do your bravest skill for family.

29

Set your next goal together — what do you want to learn?

30

🎉 Sign up for swim lessons to keep the momentum going! A certified instructor turns this confidence into real, lasting water competency. Find a warm-water, safety-first program near you.

Remember — Confidence Is Not the Same as Safety

This challenge builds comfort and early skills, but a confident child is not a "drown-proof" child. Every water day still needs a barrier, a life jacket where appropriate, an adult watching within arm's reach, and no swimming alone. The goal of these 30 days is to open the door to formal swim lessons — the single most protective step you can take.

Sources: American Academy of Pediatrics, American Red Cross, CDC. Adapt any step to your child's age and comfort; always supervise within arm's reach.

# Territory keyword research — 2026-11-03

Scope: Philadelphia and Cheltenham/Northwest Philadelphia; Howell, Brick, Toms River and the Jersey Shore; Manhattan. This is an editorial search-intent study for WWK. Sponsor status did not determine keyword selection or organic page treatment.

## Method and limits

- Reviewed final Google Search Console query/page data from February 1 through October 31, 2026.
- Sampled current local search results for each city, town and Manhattan neighborhood, including baby/toddler, parent-child, group, private, price, schedule and indoor/year-round variations.
- Reviewed the terminology and information architecture used by current local providers and parent directories.
- Mapped each intent to an existing substantial WWK page. No thin neighborhood or town pages were created.
- Search Console volumes are still very small and the new Manhattan page has no mature query history. Priorities are directional. This packet does not invent monthly search-volume, CPC or difficulty figures.

## What the evidence showed

### Philadelphia and Cheltenham

WWK already covers the core locality phrases. Search Console also surfaced parent-and-child, toddler, private, indoor/year-round and cost-related intent. Current local results consistently separate baby/toddler, group, private and year-round formats. The main Philadelphia page therefore remains the broad comparison page, with Cheltenham and Northwest Philadelphia serving their exact local intents and established age/stage pages serving narrower searches.

Examples observed in Search Console include `parent and child swimming lessons philadelphia`, `private swim lessons philadelphia`, `kids swimming lessons philadelphia`, `infant swim lessons philadelphia` and `swim lessons for toddlers philadelphia`. Current-result sources sampled included [Philadelphia magazine's local roundup](https://www.phillymag.com/be-well-philly/kids-swimming-lessons/), [Fitness Alive parent-child classes](https://fitnessalivephilly.com/babybubbles), [Fitness Alive private lessons](https://fitnessalivephilly.com/private-lessons) and [Philly J lesson formats](https://www.phillyjcc.com/swim-lessons/).

### Jersey Shore, Howell, Brick and Toms River

Exact town phrases are the clearest commercial intent. Search Console already surfaced `swim lessons brick nj`, `swim lessons toms river nj`, `howell swim lessons` and `swim lessons monmouth county nj`. Current local results emphasize infant/toddler or parent-child classes, group/private choice, year-round indoor pools, price, ratios, make-ups and current availability. The existing Jersey Shore hub now covers those decision terms and routes families to the exact town pages.

Current-result sources sampled included [NJ Swim Brick](https://njswim.com/locations/new-jersey/brick/), [Aqua-Tots Toms River](https://www.aqua-tots.com/toms-river/), [Toms River Fitness swim school](https://www.trfitness.net/swim-school/) and [Howell youth swim lessons](https://howell.ss12.sharpschool.com/community/highlander_aquatics_and_fitness_center/youth_swim_lessons).

### Manhattan

The broad target is kids swim lessons in Manhattan, but the current market is highly neighborhood-driven. Results repeatedly organize programs around the Upper East Side, Upper West Side, Midtown, Tribeca, Battery Park City and the Financial District. Parents also search by baby/toddler age, private versus group format, current availability, price and travel time. The Manhattan guide now explains these route areas using only the seven verified WWK pool addresses; it does not create unsupported neighborhood pages or include the held East Village location.

Current-result sources sampled included [SwimJim Upper East Side](https://www.swimjim.com/locations/new-york/nyc/upper-east-side-the-yorkshire-towers-swim-school), [Imagine Swimming Upper West Side](https://www.imagineswimming.com/locations/upper-west-side), [Physique Swimming Financial District](https://physiqueswimming.com/en/fidi/), [Manhattan Youth aquatics](https://manhattanyouth.org/aquatics) and the [current Manhattan toddler class index](https://enrollercoaster.com/nyc/activities/swim/manhattan/toddlers/).

## Page strategy

The structured activation and audit contract is `keyword-map.json`. It records the primary and decision-intent clusters and checks that every mapped concept remains present on the intended page. It deliberately checks concepts rather than forcing repetitive exact-match wording.

- Philadelphia: broad comparison intent on `/swim-lessons/philadelphia.html`; exact Cheltenham and Northwest Philadelphia intent on their existing primaries; age/stage intent on established toddler, beginner and kids pages.
- Jersey Shore: regional and decision intent on `/swim-lessons/jersey-shore.html`; exact town intent on Howell, Brick and Toms River primaries.
- Manhattan: borough, neighborhood-route and class-format intent on `/swim-lessons/manhattan-ny.html`; no new neighborhood pages until Search Console demand and locally distinct content justify them.

## Measurement

Use complete Search Console periods and compare query/page impressions, clicks, CTR and position by territory. New terms are hypotheses until they earn impressions. Make no more than one material content change per territory in a weekly cycle. Do not interpret rankings as a benefit of sponsorship, and do not promise a school organic placement.

## Fixed and shipped

### 1. `.tldr-box` printed on 2 checklist printables — print-rule family drift

`printable-poster.css` hides the AEO Quick Answer box in `@media print`.
`printable-checklist.css` styles `.tldr-box a` but **never hid `.tldr-box`**, so
`summer-safety-checklist-printable` and `swim-lesson-readiness-printable` were
printing the Quick Answer as page 1 — while their own source comments read
`<!-- AEO: Quick Answer (screen-only, hidden on print) -->`.

Same defect class as the styling-rule-without-floor-rule family: a variant was
added to one rule in the family and not the others.

Added `.tldr-box` to the checklist family's print `display:none` list; bumped the
cache key `20260903a → 20260903b` on all 93 pages that load the stylesheet.

  (`swimmers-hub/backstroke-complete-guide.html`). Harmless and additive, but it is
  a single-page head variant, which is the kind of thing that makes head sweeps

  future perf pass.

  block** in `<body>`. It works, but the rule belongs in the printable stylesheets

  blocks. Deferred — no user-visible defect today.

  sitemap has not been downloaded by Google since April. Both need Michael

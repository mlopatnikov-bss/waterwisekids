Above-Ground Pool Care: Setup, Water, Ladders and Storage

Care for frame, inflatable-ring and steel-wall above-ground pools: level ground, filter pumps, water testing, ladders, draining and storage.

  • Guides
  • Pool stores
  • Service companies

Applies to: Above-ground and portable pools: metal-frame, inflatable-ring (fast-set), steel-wall and small inflatable pools, with cartridge or sand filter pumps and optional plug-in chlorinators. Examples come from Bestway's support pages; your own model's manual comes first.

Quick answer

Follow your own pool's manual. For Bestway pools, for example, set up on flat, solid, level ground and size the filter pump to the pool's water capacity. Test chlorine and pH at least twice a day. Put access ladders away when adults are not present, and empty and put away small portable pools after every use.[1][2][3]

Pool upkeep is not drowning prevention.Keep the fence, self-latching gate, alarms and close adult supervision in place, and keep children away from chemicals and equipment while you work. See backyard pool safety layers.

Know what kind of pool you have

Portable pools range from tiny blow-up pools to larger designs holding thousands of gallons. A storable pool is one that can be taken apart for storage and put back together.[3][4]

Instructions differ by model, even within one brand. Bestway Hydrium pools, for example, have no drain valve, while other Bestway models do. Keep your owner's manual and follow it over any general advice.[5]

Check that add-on equipment suits your pool. For example, do not use Hayward H150FD–H500FD heaters with storable pools.[4]

Start with level, solid ground

  • For Bestway pools, almost any completely flat, solid, level surface can work. The ground must be stable enough to hold the weight of the water.[1]
  • Do not set up a Bestway pool on driveways, decks, platforms, gravel or asphalt, and do not use sand for leveling: it can shift under the pool.[1]
  • If a Bestway pool bulges on one side, it is not on level ground, which places undue stress on the seams. Empty the pool and move it to a level site.[1]
  • Bestway pools are designed to sit above ground, not buried.[1]
  • Fill a Bestway pool until the wall stands straight and the water reaches the welding line. Do not overfill: this could make the pool collapse.[1]

Filter pumps: match, maintain, unplug

For Bestway pools, size the filtration system to the pool's water capacity, and before using a pump on a new pool, check that its flow rate suits that pool.[1]

  • Cartridge filter pumps: on Bestway pumps, you can rinse the cartridge with a garden hose and reuse it, but replace it if it stays dirty and discolored.[6]
  • Sand filter pumps: on Bestway's sand filters, a gauge reading in the red means the sand is full of debris and needs a backwash. Backwashing lowers the water level, so the pool may need topping up.[6]
  • Purge air from Bestway Flowclear filter pumps before first use and after any maintenance.[6]
  • Do not use a Bestway pool while its sand filter pump is running. Check what your own manual says.[6]

SafetyUnplug before you touch it. On Bestway equipment, unplug the pool from the outlet before working on the chlorinator's electrolytic cell, and unplug the skimmer's motor before any maintenance. For Hayward H150FD–H500FD heaters, turn the power supply off before installing or servicing the heater. Bestway pumps are not recommended for use during a storm when thunder or lightning is present. Check your own equipment's manual.[4][6]

Water care for a smaller pool

A Bestway filter pump alone cannot keep water hygienic; chemical products are still needed. Keep pH between 7.0 and 7.8 and chlorine at least 1 ppm in pools, or at least 2 ppm if you use cyanuric acid, a chlorine stabilizer. Test chlorine and pH at least twice a day, and follow your test maker's directions.[2][6]

Overusing chemicals can damage Bestway PVC liners. On those pools, follow the suggested quantity and use a chemical floater. Dose only as the product label directs for your pool's volume.[6][7]

SafetyNever mix chlorine products with acid; this could create toxic gases. Lock chemicals away from children and animals. If a label directs pre-dissolving, add the chemical to water, never water to the chemical. For an exposure, call Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222.[7][8][9]

Ladders, climbing and barriers

  • Cover larger portable pools and put access ladders away when adults are not present.[3]
  • From the Hayward H150FD–H500FD heater manual: do not let children use or climb on heaters, pumps or filters, and these components must be positioned so children cannot use them to get into the pool.[4]
  • Fence portable pools and install alarms on doors leading to the pool area.[3]
  • Never jump or dive into a Bestway pool.[1]

SafetyOnce a portable pool is set up, ensure high levels of supervision. Designate a Water Watcher, an adult whose job is to supervise children in the water.[3][10]

Small inflatable pools

Empty and put away smaller portable pools after every use. See our inflatable pool safety page for more.[3]

Draining and storing a seasonal pool

For Bestway above-ground pools, taking the pool down for winter is strongly recommended, because ice and snow can collapse it. Do it when temperatures fall below 45°F (8°C). APX 365 pools are different: Polar-Shield year-round weather defense lets the pool stay up in winter without completely emptying it, and the manual gives winterizing steps for APX 365 pools only. Follow your own model's manual.[6][11]

  1. Drain where the water can go safely, following your manual. On Bestway pools, put the hose end in an area where the water can be safely drained. Check local regulations on pool water discharge first. Rules vary by place; in Southern Nevada, failing to discharge pool drainage into a public sanitary sewer, if available, counts as water waste.[5][12][13]
  2. Clean a Bestway pool with a mild soap solution and rinse it well.[5]
  3. Dry it completely before storing.[5]
  4. Fold and store as your manual says. Fold a Bestway liner, for example, only above 50°F (10°C), and store the pool away from chemicals, rodents and children.[5][6]

When to call for help

When to call a professionalOn Bestway pools, you do not need to empty the pool to fix a liner hole, and when a hose connection leaks, check the seal gasket. If a Bestway pool's wall bulges, empty it and move it to a level site. If you cannot find a leak yourself, consider a professional plumber. On a Hayward H150FD–H500FD heater, licensed electricians must do all electrical wiring. For a chemical exposure, call Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222.[1][4][6][9][14]

Common questions

Do I really need to remove the ladder?
Yes. Cover larger portable pools and put access ladders away when adults are not present.[3]
Can I set my pool on sand or a deck?
Not a Bestway pool. Do not use sand to level a Bestway pool, and do not set it up on decks, platforms, gravel or asphalt. Check your own manual.[1]
Can I run the filter and skip chemicals?
For a Bestway filter pump, no. The filter traps particles, but chemical products are needed to keep the water hygienic.[6]

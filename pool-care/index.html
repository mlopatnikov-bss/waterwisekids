Pool Care for Families

Sourced guides to looking after a home pool through the year, plus pool stores and pool service companies listed by state.

  • Guides
  • Pool stores
  • Service companies

Practical, sourced guides for families who look after a home pool: in-ground or above-ground, vinyl, fiberglass or plaster, chlorine or salt. Each guide says what government health agencies and equipment makers advise, and links to the official source. Your own equipment manual and product labels come first, because products differ.

Pool upkeep is not drowning prevention.Keep the fence, self-latching gate, alarms and close adult supervision in place, and keep children away from chemicals and equipment while you work. See backyard pool safety layers.

Find local help

Pool stores sell supplies and chemicals, and many test water. Pool service companies maintain or repair home pools. They are separate lists: a store is only listed as a service company if its own website says it services home pools.

How to Become a Swim Instructor

To become a swim instructor, earn a Water Safety Instructor (WSI) certification from the American Red Cross or YMCA. You must be at least 16, a strong swimmer, and hold CPR/AED and first aid certification. The 2–3 day course costs $150–$250 and is valid for 3 years.

Quick Answer: Become a swim instructor by earning a Red Cross or YMCA Water Safety Instructor (WSI) certification at age 16+. You'll need strong swimming ability plus CPR/AED and first aid. Training takes 2–3 days, costs $150–$250, and most instructors earn $17–$24 per hour.

What Do You Need to Become a Swim Instructor?

Minimum Requirements:
  • Age 14-16+ (varies by organization)
  • Strong swimming ability (comfortable in all pool areas)
  • CPR/AED Certification
  • WSI (Water Safety Instructor) Certification from Red Cross or YMCA
  • First Aid Certification
  • Patience and communication skills

Which Swim Instructor Certification Should You Get?

According to the American Red Cross, Water Safety Instructor candidates must be at least 16 and able to demonstrate Learn-to-Swim Level 4 swimming skills before the course.

Red Cross Water Safety Instructor (WSI): 2-3 day course, $150-200, valid for 3 years. Most common certification.

YMCA Swim Instructor: Similar to Red Cross, 2-3 days, $200-250, valid 3 years.

Prerequisite: Usually requires Lifeguard Training first (or concurrent certification).

What Is the Swim Instructor Career Path?

  1. Get Lifeguard certified (if not already)
  2. Get WSI certified (Water Safety Instructor)
  3. Work as swim instructor ($17-22/hr)
  4. Advance to senior instructor or head coach (supervisor)
  5. Move into aquatics management

Where Do Swim Instructors Work?

  • Public pools and YMCAs ($17-22/hr)
  • Private swim schools ($18-24/hr)
  • Waterparks ($18-26/hr)
  • High schools and colleges
  • Resorts and hotels
  • Military and government

Frequently Asked Questions

What certification do you need to teach swimming?

Most swim instructors hold a Water Safety Instructor (WSI) certification from the American Red Cross or an equivalent YMCA swim instructor certification. CPR/AED and first aid certifications are also typically required.

How old do you have to be to become a swim instructor?

American Red Cross Water Safety Instructor candidates must be at least 16 years old by the last day of the course and able to demonstrate Learn-to-Swim Level 4 swimming skills. Some assistant instructor roles start at 14 to 15.

Do you need to be a lifeguard before becoming a swim instructor?

Not always, but lifeguard training is frequently required or recommended, either as a prerequisite or completed at the same time. Lifeguard certification makes you more employable and prepares you for water emergencies.

How much do swim instructors make?

Swim instructors generally earn $17 to $24 per hour. Public pools and YMCAs pay around $17 to $22, private swim schools $18 to $24, and waterparks $18 to $26.

Can you become a swim instructor without experience?

Yes. Many swim schools actively hire and train instructors without prior teaching experience. Strong swimming ability, patience, and good communication skills often matter more than experience.

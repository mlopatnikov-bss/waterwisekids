Swimming Scholarships for Teens

Yes, teen swimmers can earn college scholarships: NCAA Division I and II programs, NAIA schools, and junior colleges offer athletic awards from partial aid to full tuition, based on qualifying times and academics. Start building your recruiting profile in the first year of high school.

Quick Answer: Swimming scholarships come from NCAA Division I and II colleges, NAIA schools, junior colleges, and grants such as those from USA Swimming. Division III schools do not give athletic scholarships but may offer academic and need-based aid. Begin early, keep your GPA strong, and contact coaches directly.

What Do College Swimming Scholarships Require?

Many colleges offer full or partial scholarships for competitive swimmers. Requirements typically include:

  • Competitive swimming experience (club or high school team)
  • Qualifying times in your events
  • Good academic standing (GPA 3.0+)
  • Strong character and leadership

NCAA Division I, II, and III

Major colleges compete in NCAA swimming. Division I and II programs can award athletic scholarships, from partial awards to full tuition; Division III does not award athletic scholarships but offers academic and need-based aid. The process begins freshman year of high school. See the NCAA and USA Swimming for official eligibility and program information.

Club Swim Team Scholarships

Some elite club teams offer scholarships based on financial need and competitive level. Contact local clubs for availability.

Community College Swimming

JUCO swimming offers more scholarships, easier path to D1/D2 schools. Good option if GPA or times aren't quite Division I level yet.

What Financial Aid and Grants Are Available for Swimmers?

  • Swimming Foundation grants for talented swimmers with financial need
  • USA Swimming national and regional scholarship programs
  • State-specific aquatics education grants
  • Private foundation scholarships for swimmers

How Do You Get Recruited to Swim in College?

Start in your first year of high school. Build your profile through competitive swimming, attend recruiting showcases, communicate with coaches, and maintain strong academics.

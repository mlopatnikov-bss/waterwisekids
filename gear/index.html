Best Goggles for Kids & Swim Gear Guide 2026

Goggle picks by age from certified swim instructors, plus the rest of the gear that actually matters. No fluff.

What are the best goggles for kids?

The best goggles for kids are the ones that seal on your child’s face without being pulled tight. For ages 3–5, pick soft one-piece silicone goggles with a fabric or split strap. For ages 6–10, pick a kids’ frame with a quick-adjust strap and anti-fog lenses. From about age 10, or once your child joins a swim team, move to a junior racing goggle. Fit matters more than brand.

The 5-second fit test (do it in the store or at home)

  1. With the strap off, press the goggles gently onto your child’s eyes.
  2. If they hold on their own for a few seconds, the seal is right. If they fall off, try another size or nose bridge.
  3. Put the strap on at ear height, snug but not tight. Red rings that last more than about 20 minutes after swimming mean the strap is too tight.

Replace goggles every season for regular swimmers, or sooner if the gaskets stop sealing or the strap is at its last notch. Many instructors start beginners without goggles so children learn to stay calm with water on their face, so keep some goggle-free practice too. For a step-by-step buying guide, read how to choose swim goggles for kids, and use the kids’ swim gear fit checklist before each new session.

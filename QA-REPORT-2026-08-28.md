## Violations found and fixed

`209ee00` added the Swim Lesson Format Decision Worksheet landing page to the sitemap
(`/education/` URLs went 415 → 416) but left the hardcoded counts at 414.

Source of truth: `grep -c '/education/' sitemap.xml` − 1 = **415**. Verified the formula against
history — at `90d07a8` the sitemap held 415 and the pages said 414.

### 2. Three residual endorsement claims

`cfabf5e` retired unsupported CDC/AAP/Red Cross endorsement language earlier the same day, but
only on the pages it touched. A sitewide sweep for the same claim shape found three more, none
of them mirrored anywhere else:

- **`education/pediatricians-guide-swim-lessons.html`** — "layered protection … is the strategy
  **endorsed by** both the AAP and the American Red Cross" → "**independently recommended by**".
  Neither body endorses a jointly named strategy; each publishes its own layered guidance.
- **`education/free-reduced-swim-lessons-make-a-splash.html`** — "a widely cited study **endorsed
  by** the American Academy of Pediatrics" → "a widely cited study **that the AAP cites in its
  water-safety guidance**". The AAP cites the 88% study; it does not endorse studies.
- **`education/water-safety-myths.html`** — Article JSON-LD `articleBody` read "debunks 10 critical
  water safety myths **backed by** CDC, AAP, and Red Cross research" → "**drawing on**". The old
  phrasing also parsed as *the myths* being CDC-backed. JSON-LD re-validated after the edit
  (3 blocks, all parse).

### 3. Sitemap lastmod

Bumped only the three pages with visible content changes (`/`, `/about/`,
`pediatricians-guide-swim-lessons`; the make-a-splash page was already at today's date).
`water-safety-myths` was **not** bumped — its change was schema-only, not a payload change.
Sitemap re-validated as well-formed XML. No blanket bump.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Effective Date: October 8, 2026. We may update this policy periodically. Please check back for changes.

Introduction

WaterWiseKids ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We are a content site focused on water safety education and swim lesson discovery. We do not operate complex user account systems or advanced data collection practices.

What Information We Collect

Here's what we collect and how:

  • Email Inquiries: When you contact us via email or our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any message content you choose to share. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • No User Accounts: We do not require users to create accounts or log in.
  • Site Analytics: We use Google Tag Manager (GTM) to help us understand how visitors use our site, such as which pages are most popular. This service may use cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Aquatic Jobs submissions

When an employer submits a role, we collect the job details, application link or application email, and the submitter’s name, email and optional telephone number. We use these details to review and manage the submission. Approved job details and the employer’s chosen application link or application email appear publicly. The submitter’s private contact details and management code do not appear in public listings. Applicants apply directly to the employer; WaterWiseKids does not collect applications through this board.

Submissions are stored in a private Google spreadsheet. A private backend uses the site owner’s Google authorization to read and update that spreadsheet. Visitors do not grant Google account access. Google authorization credentials remain in encrypted backend secrets; the site does not use this authorization to read unrelated spreadsheets or for advertising. The Google permission itself covers spreadsheets accessible to the authorizing owner, even though this backend pins one Jobs spreadsheet.

Cloudflare hosts the protected submission gateway and checks submissions using Turnstile. The gateway uses the connection IP address to derive a keyed hash for request limits; it does not save the raw IP address in its application rate-limit database. Cloudflare and Google process requests under their own service privacy policies. A job’s application email is public only when the employer chooses it as the application contact.

Employers can use their management code to edit or withdraw their submission. Edited roles return to review. Withdrawn, rejected, closed and expired rows become eligible for removal after 30 days. The current implementation requires the owner to run the purge action; automatic deletion has not been installed. Requests for access, correction or deletion can be sent to waterwisekids.com@gmail.com.

How We Use Your Information

WaterWiseKids may link to third-party websites and resources (such as swim school partner sites, water safety organizations, or educational materials). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party site before providing your information or using their services.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information to WaterWiseKids should contact us immediately at waterwisekids.com@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect information submitted to us via email and contact forms. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

  • Request correction of inaccurate information

  • Opt out of future communications from us

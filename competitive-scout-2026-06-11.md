# Competitive Scout Report — KIDS FIRST Swim Schools
**Date:** 2026-06-11 (17th chain scouted; first since SwimRight 2026-04-27)
**Why this target:** Largest swim chain headquartered in our partner's home region — 7 PA locations (Jenkintown, Plymouth Meeting, Broomall, Aston, Exton, Limerick, York) ring Northwest Philadelphia, plus Cherry Hill NJ near the Jersey Shore partner. Direct local competitor to British Swim School in both partner markets.

## Company snapshot
- Founded 2001 in Maryland by Gary Roth (40+ yr swim coach, ex-Fortune 500 exec); HQ Fallston, MD. Franchising since 2010 (first franchises 2011: Dundalk MD, Philadelphia PA, Charleston SC). Corporate + franchise hybrid.
- Claims to be "the world's largest provider of children's swimming instruction" (~21 listed locations: 10 MD, 7 PA, 2 VA, 1 NJ, 1 SC). The claim is per-students-taught, not locations — unverifiable as published.
- Retail strip-mall facilities (e.g., Jenkintown = Cloverly Plaza Shopping Center) — same retail-conversion model as BSS pool-rental inverse: they build small warm-water pools inside shopping centers.
- USA Swimming Foundation partner badge in footer.
- Site quality note: their WordPress Revolution Slider is serving injected ads for "Window Depot USA of Baltimore" on every page header — either a compromised/misconfigured plugin or sold ad space; either way it signals web neglect. Their education/blog content is essentially nonexistent — among the thinnest content footprints of all 17 chains scouted.

## Curriculum & pedagogy
- **KIDS FIRST / Kick FIRST®** — 8 copyrighted skill levels, Level 1 (blowing bubbles) → Level 8 (swim 50 yards). They openly tell parents the journey "takes several years."
- **Kick-first thesis:** human locomotion is 1-1 arm/leg timing; swimming is 1-6 (six-beat kick per arm cycle), which can't be consciously coordinated — so train the kick to subconscious automaticity BEFORE introducing arms. This is a *stroke-mechanics-first* philosophy, the opposite pole from survival-first programs (ISR, BSS, SwimRight).
- Heavily markets that the curriculum was "awarded copyright protection by the United States Library of Congress in 2008" — framing routine copyright registration as a quality credential. No efficacy data published.
- Report cards issued each session; instructor/manager evaluation gates advancement ("don't rush levels").
- Curriculum page = 6 YouTube videos (Mommy & Me through Level 5). No written skill benchmarks published for levels 6–8.

## Policies (consumer-relevant fine print)
- Monthly autopay billing; 30-day notice to cancel; **no refunds, ever** ("once a spot is sold, it cannot be resold... KIDS FIRST cannot assume that risk on your behalf").
- **Makeups: group/semi-private lessons get NO makeups** — substitute is a Family Swim pass (2 hrs pool time). Parent & Me: makeup within same month. Private: 1 per session. This is the stingiest group-makeup policy in our 17-chain library (vs Emler 12/yr, LPB ~8/yr, Swimtastic 4/cycle).
- Rolling monthly enrollment, join anytime; starts at 6 months recommended (P&Me listed from 2 months).
- Special needs served via private lessons only — no named adaptive program (vs S.N.A.P., T.A.P., Swim Whisperers).
- Splash Parties / birthday rentals at all locations; Family Swims at all locations.

## Competitive assessment vs. British Swim School (our partner)
**KIDS FIRST strengths:** lower group price point ($30/lesson), owned facilities with consistent warm water, homeschool niche, semi-private value tier, swim-team pathway, 7-location PA density.
**KIDS FIRST weaknesses we can highlight (neutrally, via education content):** stroke-first ≠ survival-first — no survival/water-safety sequence in their published curriculum at all (BSS leads with survival); 15-family Parent & Me classes; no group makeups; no refunds; no named adaptive program; near-zero water-safety educational content; multi-year progression openly admitted; copyright marketed as credential.

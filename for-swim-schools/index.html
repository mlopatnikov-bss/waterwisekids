Get Your Swim School in Front of Local Families

WaterWiseKids helps families find quality swim programs. We'll build you a dedicated profile page, feature your school on our Find Lessons directory, and drive traffic through our water safety education content.

Quick answer: Listing your swim school on WaterWiseKids is free. We build you a dedicated profile page, add your school to a directory of 799 verified swim schools across 573 towns and cities in all 50 states, and link families to you from more than 400 water safety guides. Families contact you directly — we never sit between you and the enquiry, and we don't take a cut of enrolments. Schools that want extra visibility can add a paid featured placement.

How Do Families Actually Find a Swim School?

Almost every enrolment now starts with a search. Parents type things like “how much do swim lessons cost in [their town]” or “top rated kids swim schools near me” — and increasingly they read an AI-generated summary before they ever click a website. That changes what a swim school needs in order to be found: not just a listing, but a listing on a page that search engines and AI assistants already trust to answer local swim-lesson questions.

That is the specific gap WaterWiseKids fills. Our directory pages are built around the questions families actually ask — cost, class ratios, instructor certification, water temperature, and how to judge whether a school is genuinely good — so they surface for local searches that a single school's own website rarely wins on its own.

Common Questions

What does it cost?

A directory listing and profile page are free. Schools that want featured placement can choose a paid package. Founding partners keep their free profile as the platform grows.

Do we need to be part of a specific franchise?

No. We list independent schools, franchises and community programs. The directory is curated, though: we do not list every school that applies, and in some areas we list only selected schools.

What does "founding partner" mean?

It means you're among the first schools on the platform. You get input on features, and your profile stays listed at no cost as the site grows — you're grandfathered in.

How do families reach us through the site?

Your profile page includes your phone number, website link, and any booking links you provide. Families contact you directly — we don't sit in the middle.

How quickly can we get listed?

Most profiles go live within a week of initial contact. We'll need your program details, service areas, and any photos or credentials you'd like included.

How many swim schools are listed on WaterWiseKids?

As of October 2026 the directory holds 799 verified swim schools across 573 towns and cities in all 50 states and Washington, D.C. Listings are checked against each operator's own published location list before they go live, and the directory grows every week.

Will a listing help my swim school get found on Google?

That is the point of it. Our state and town pages are built to answer the local questions families search — what lessons cost, which schools are top rated, what class ratios and certifications to expect — and your profile sits inside those answers. A listing does not replace your own website; it gives you a second, well-ranked page working for you on searches a single school site rarely wins alone.

Do you sell our enquiries as leads to other schools?

No. We do not resell enquiries, charge per lead, or let another school pay to appear on your profile. Families see your phone number, website and booking link and contact you directly.

Can we update our programs, hours or pricing later?

Yes, at any time and at no cost. Email the changes and we will update your profile — most edits are live the same week. Keeping your programs and service areas current is the single biggest thing that makes a listing convert.

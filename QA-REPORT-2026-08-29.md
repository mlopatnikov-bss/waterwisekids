## Defects found and fixed — VGBA scope residuals

Yesterday's CPSC scout commit (`da0be27`) corrected Virginia Graeme Baker Act scope on 5 pages. **The same claim shape survived on 6 pages it did not touch.** A shape sweep across all 25 pages mentioning the Act — visible prose *and* JSON-LD — found 9 residuals.

### P1 — Federal mandate asserted where it does not reach

**4 instances, 2 pages:**

- `education/pool-drain-entrapment-safety-checklist.html` ×3 — *"The Virginia Graeme Baker Act applies to all spas and hot tubs, public or private."* Present in the FAQ JSON-LD (line 105) **and** twice in visible prose (189, 233).
- `education/pool-drain-safety.html` ×1 — *"…whether public or private."* This page **was** touched by `da0be27`; the residual survived inside it.

VGBA (15 U.S.C. § 8003) defines "public pool and spa" as one open to the public, to members of an organization, to residents of a multi-unit apartment building or complex, to hotel patrons, or federally operated. Single-family residential pools and spas sit outside the federal facility mandate. Verified against CPSC and Congress.gov, not memory alone.

**3 pages, homeowner-facing, calling the covers legally required:**

- `education/family-pool-safety-checklist.html` — *"anti-entrapment drain covers are required under the Virginia Graeme Baker Act (2008)"*
- `education/water-safety-month-action-plan.html` — *"have been legally required … since 2008"*
- `education/water-safety-month-action-plan-printable.html` — *"are legally required under the Virginia Graeme Baker Act (2008)"*

All three address backyard pool owners directly. Rewritten to name the covered categories (apartment, condo, HOA, hotel, other public pools) and mark backyard use as best practice rather than federal law — keeping the safety advice intact while removing the false legal claim.

### P2 — Year-label drift

- `education/grandparent-water-safety-checklist.html` ×2 (JSON-LD + prose) cited *"(Virginia Graeme Baker Act, 2008)"* against the "2007 Act" naming used on 20 other pages. Now *"signed 2007 and effective 2008"* — both valid numbers, each labeled with its period.

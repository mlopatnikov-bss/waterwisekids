Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a swim school near me?

Search by city or ZIP code, then filter by age group, program type, price, and location. WaterWiseKids lists swim schools across all 50 states with verified parent ratings so you can compare options in your area.

What makes a good swim school?

Look for certified instructors, small class sizes, clean and well-maintained facilities, and a clear focus on water-safety skills. Verified parent reviews and transparent pricing also help you judge quality before enrolling.

What age should kids start swim lessons?

The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends most children start formal swim lessons after their first birthday, when increasing mobility raises the risk of unintended water access. The AAP cites research showing formal lessons can reduce drowning risk by 88% for young children.

Are swim school reviews on WaterWiseKids verified?

WaterWiseKids collects parent ratings and reviews to help families compare swim schools. Reviews reflect parent experiences and are meant to guide your research alongside a direct visit or call to the school.
